// 02 · 2025–2026 Flagship Projects

Applied Research

Real-World Quantum Solutions.

Explore current initiatives built, modeled, and tested end-to-end by student engineering leads at Western.

Amir Orfali · Lead [QAE / QISKIT]

Quantum Advantage in Quant Finance

Investigating Quantum Amplitude Estimation (QAE) to achieve quadratic speedup over classical nested Monte Carlo simulations for derivative pricing and multi-asset risk analysis.

Project breakdown ↗
Nicolas Folz · Lead [ANNEALING]

Last-Mile Delivery Optimization

Modeling NP-hard vehicle routing constraints using quantum annealing algorithms, benchmarking speed and route efficiency against standard classical heuristics.

Project breakdown ↗
Victor Postea · Lead [POST-QUANTUM CRYPTO]

QModelGuard: AI Asset Protection

Securing high-value machine learning weights from harvest-now-decrypt-later attacks using post-quantum cryptographic libraries, lattice-based encryption, and digital signatures.

Project breakdown ↗
Hunter Korble · Lead [HARDWARE / OPTICS]

Physical Double-Slit Experiment

Constructing a physical laboratory apparatus to demonstrate wave-particle duality, photon interference fringes, and quantum measurement phenomena with real-time digital detection.
