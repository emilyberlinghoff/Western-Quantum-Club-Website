HardHaQ 2026: Hardware & Quantum Hackathon

Western Quantum Club's flagship 48-hour hackathon. Dual tracks: (1) Quantum Software (solving optimization and finance problems using IBM Qiskit and quantum annealing) and (2) Quantum Hardware (physical optics experiments, lasers, and detector engineering). Includes workshops, industry mentors, and hardware prizes.

Certification · 2025
Winter 2025 • Spencer Engineering Labs

IBM Qiskit Developer Certification Program

A structured study cohort designed to guide Western students toward earning the official IBM Certified Associate Developer credential for Quantum Computation using Qiskit. Led by VP Software Education Emily Berlinghoff, covering Dirac bra-ket algebra, multi-qubit gates, Bell states, and quantum error mitigation.
