Upcoming 2026 • Hackathon / Competition • Amit Chakma Engineering Building (ACEB)

HardHaQ 2026: Western Hardware & Quantum Hackathon

Western Quantum Club's flagship 48-hour hackathon bringing together students across engineering, physics, and computer science. Features dual tracks: (1) Quantum Software (solving optimization and finance problems using IBM Qiskit and quantum annealing) and (2) Quantum Hardware (physical optics experiments, lasers, and detector engineering). Includes workshops, industry mentors, and hardware prizes.

IBM Qiskit Quantum Annealing Optics Hardware Prizes & Mentorship
Winter 2025 – 2026 • Certification Cohort • Spencer Engineering Labs & Discord

IBM Qiskit Associate Developer Certification Program

A semester-long, structured study group designed to guide Western students toward earning the official IBM Certified Associate Developer credential for Quantum Computation using Qiskit. Led by VP Software Education Emily Berlinghoff, the cohort covers Dirac bra-ket algebra, multi-qubit gates, Bell states, quantum teleportation, phase estimation, and quantum error mitigation.

IBM Certified Developer Weekly Mentorship Hands-on Labs
Fall 2024 – Winter 2025 • Workshop Series • Western Engineering Classrooms

Quantum Software & Theory Workshop Series

Bi-weekly interactive workshops bridging the gap between introductory physics and production code. Sessions covered quantum mechanics fundamentals, moving beyond gate-level coding, exploring high-level abstractions, and simulating real-world quantum use cases in investment banking and logistics optimization.

Python & Qiskit Algorithm Design Open to All Disciplines
March 2024 • Faculty Partnership • Western AI Systems Engineering

WQC x Dr. Shaimaa Ali: Quantum AI Systems Initiative

An inaugural partnership event with Dr. Shaimaa Ali, Inaugural Director of the AI Systems Engineering Program at Western University. The collaboration brought together machine learning and quantum computing enthusiasts to investigate quantum neural networks, post-quantum data security, and curriculum integration for Western students.

Quantum AI AI Systems Engineering Faculty Keynote
March 24, 2023 • Outreach & Demo • Amit Chakma Engineering Building (ACEB)

WQC Spring Engineering Open House

The club welcomed hundreds of incoming engineering students and parents at the ACEB Atrium. Club executives set up interactive quantum circuit demos, answered questions on quantum ethics and commercial timelines, and distributed educational pamphlets on how students can prepare for future quantum careers.

Live Demos Community Outreach ACEB Showcase
Fall 2022 • Club Launch • Western University Main Campus

Western Quantum Club Inaugural General Meeting

Founders Jackson Freeman, Emma Turk, and Nicolas Folz launched the Western Quantum Club to empower students with the skills required to lead the next quantum revolution. Established the core subteams: Computing Applications, Research & Advisory, Consulting, and Operations.
